# Symphony Backtesting Vector intent

SBV lets users define, inspect and evolve quantitative experiments. User intent controls criteria, fitting, weighting, metrics, studies, replay retention and optimization goals. Defaults are explicit replaceable profiles, not platform truth. All implemented capability has an agentic qxctl control plane and portable evidence usable by future GUIs and external AI/ML tools.

The enduring design distinguishes signal opportunity, likelihood of execution, economic outcomes and actual execution. A fast C++26 multi-pass architecture should make these distinctions inspectable while supporting alternative user-specified modes. Performance claims require reproducible benchmarks. CUDA/tensor capability contracts may precede their integrations and must disclose actual availability.
