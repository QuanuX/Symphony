# Symphony Intelligence Vector Intent

## Purpose

The Symphony Intelligence Vector (SIV) owns future agentic collaboration, context, communication, and governed interaction with Symphony.

## Naming

SAV remains the Symphony Accordare Vector. The earlier use of SAV for an agentic vector is superseded by SIV. SAIV is reserved for a later Symphony intelligence-integration sub-vector and has no current behavior.

## Scope

SIV is expected to support local and remote agents, extended local context, structured long-term logic, inter-agent communication, and human-directed operation at the Symphony surface. SMCV is its first named optional component.

## Non-Scope

SIV does not grant authority based on caller type, replace vector-owned semantics, become canonical truth, enter the trading hot path, or make the older artificial-intelligence working paper conclusive.
