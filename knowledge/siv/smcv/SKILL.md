# Symphony Markdown Conversion Vector Skill

## Procedure

1. Identify the exact source and destination contract versions.
2. Require a ratified lossless mapping before conversion.
3. Preserve values, types, bounds, identifiers, errors, and critical unknowns.
4. Keep conversion distinct from validation, authorization, transmission, and execution.
5. Respect the user-selected qxctl enablement and scope.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before inventing Markdown grammar, mapping defaults, field repair, lossy normalization, qxctl command names, transport, persistence, or hot-path use.
