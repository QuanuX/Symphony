# Symphony Ops Vector Skill

## Purpose

Guide agents and implementers through SOV work without converting operational possibilities into platform doctrine.

## Reading Order

1. `knowledge/ARCHITECTURE.md`
2. `knowledge/SLANG.md`
3. `knowledge/NAMESPACES.md`
4. `knowledge/sov/INTENT.md`
5. `knowledge/sov/MANIFEST.md`
6. `knowledge/sov/SPEC.md`
7. `knowledge/sov/REMOTE-QXCTL.md` or `knowledge/sov/PRIMA-PARTE.md` when applicable
8. the exact SCV, SNV, SHV, SKV lifecycle, SSIAG, STAV, SSFV, SACV, and SODV contracts implicated by the proposed operation

## Procedure

1. Identify the exact requested operation and target scope.
2. Separate user-supplied choices from observed evidence and unresolved facts.
3. Locate the lowest semantic owner for every input and consequence.
4. Preserve provider, hardware, transport, and bus neutrality.
5. Define expected state, idempotency, version compatibility, verification, failure, interruption, and recovery before implementation.
6. Add qxctl grammar only after the SOV operation contract is ratified.
7. Add SSFV, invariant, lifecycle, SSIAG/STAV, SKVI, SCLV, and SODV consequences only where they truly apply.

## Stop Conditions

Stop for Architect review before selecting one remote-management technology, defining strategy behavior, inventing a provider preference, enabling a persistent Node listener, introducing a container layer, widening a Habitat without user intent, or implementing an unratified mutation.
