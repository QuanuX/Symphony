# Symphony Ops Vector Intent

## Purpose

The Symphony Ops Vector (SOV) owns the operational contract by which Symphony uses qxctl to influence user-selected infrastructure and deliver exact Node, Habitat, Nest, and bus-adapter changes.

## Scope

SOV covers provider and owned-hardware provisioning, Habitat conditioning, Nest delivery and update, bus-adapter establishment, optional remote-Node administration, and the exact compatibility evidence required before an operation is attempted.

## User Authority

The user selects providers, regions, hardware, buses, software, deployment topology, and per-Node administration arrangements. SOV exposes and executes supported choices; it does not rank them or convert recommendations into mandatory policy.

## Non-Scope

SOV does not own strategy logic, cloud/provider semantic knowledge, physical Node identity, hardware-capability knowledge, official publication truth, or canonical knowledge mutation. It does not require remote management or continuous Node-local residency.

## Relationships

- qxctl is the Go agentic-first command surface through which ratified SOV operations are administered.
- SCV supplies provider/offsite capability and constraint knowledge.
- SNV supplies Node, resource, cluster, and name identity evidence.
- SHV may later supply detailed physical hardware capability evidence.
- SKV governs SOV's canonical contracts, change consequences, indexing, and lifecycle integration.
- SODV governs any official public projection of SOV documentation.
