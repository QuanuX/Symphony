# SEV STAV Integration

SEV has no registered STAV producer tuple in v1. The engine is report-only and proposal-only; impact assessment, disposition planning, graph projection, command-surface assessment, novelty validation, watch-policy checking, trigger coalescing, and lifecycle binding do not become audit events merely because they execute.

SSIAG decisions and any external lifecycle mutation retain their own audit ownership. Voluntary novelty export remains unimplemented and requires its own operation semantics, safe-metadata review, producer boundary, partial-delivery behavior, and exact grant before a tuple can be added.

Absence is intentional and fail-closed: configuration cannot invent a SEV event class, operation, intent, target kind, or reason code.
