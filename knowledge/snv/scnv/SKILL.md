# Symphony Consolidated Naming Vector Skill

## Procedure

1. Resolve the subject through SNIV, SNRV, or SCIV before consolidating names.
2. Preserve every name's source, scope, lifecycle, and qualification.
3. Apply TOPS, TROG, cluster, and Node qualification without imposing a user naming style.
4. Reject ambiguous active resolution.
5. Distinguish retired-name reuse from simultaneous collision.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before issuing a name, defining universal namespace policy, inferring physical identity from a nickname, or inventing a machine grammar or resolver operation.
