# Symphony Consolidated Naming Vector Manifest

## Canonical Target

`knowledge/snv/scnv/`

## Declared Contract Truth Role

SCNV owns SNV-bounded name association and resolution. Root `knowledge/NAMESPACES.md` owns universal namespace-family delegation; individual identity owners allocate their own identities.

## Canonical Surfaces

- `knowledge/snv/scnv/INTENT.md`
- `knowledge/snv/scnv/MANIFEST.md`
- `knowledge/snv/scnv/SPEC.md`
- `knowledge/snv/scnv/SKILL.md`

## Implementation Status

Canonical contract only. No naming registry, resolver, alias service, engine, or qxctl command is implemented.

## Non-Authorization Statement

SCNV records and resolves names supplied through their owners. It does not issue, approve, reject, or automatically change a name.
