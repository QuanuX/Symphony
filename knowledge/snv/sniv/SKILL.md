# Symphony Node Identity Vector Skill

## Procedure

1. Identify the physical subject and every supplied identity domain.
2. Separate provider, infrastructure, Symphony, user, boot, Habitat, and incarnation facts.
3. Determine whether an observed change is software-only, remotely attached, or a material local hardware change.
4. Preserve predecessor and retirement evidence without pretending continuity.
5. Return unresolved when the evidence cannot establish one subject.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before inventing identifier syntax, evidence precedence, automatic discovery, provider ownership, or incarnation cardinality.
