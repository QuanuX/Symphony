## Required semantic distinctions

- Physical Node continuity, participation incarnation, boot, Habitat, resource attachment, membership, observed fabric and name association remain different facts.
- A caller selects a versioned material-hardware profile. Temporary disconnect/reconnect preserves an incarnation until explicit end/rejoin.
- Cluster findings require at least two identified participating Nodes and the selected connectivity profile. Multiple buses may support one cluster; an unproved path switching fabrics establishes no connection.
- Original attributed evidence and proposed typed transitions remain available for replay. Missing, partial, stale, conflicting and absent-time evidence retain uncertainty.
- Pure validation/planning, private evidence retention and protected selected-view effects have separate contracts. SSIAG authorizes selection and STAV audits the actual policy decision; no head-write STAV receipt is fabricated.
