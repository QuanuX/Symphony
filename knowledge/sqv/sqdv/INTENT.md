# Symphony Quantitative Delivery Vector Intent

## Purpose

The Symphony Quantitative Delivery Vector (SQDV) owns the selected research-data view and handoff to an authorized consumer beneath SQV. It defines which dataset and revision, representation, completeness level, recipient scope, and delivery mode that consumer receives.

## Composition Boundary

Users may select independent research, backtest, intelligence, presentation, or later external recipients. Each view uses the actual receiving contract and its own finite allowance. A disposable preview, a committed research view, and a retained catch-up view have different evidence and obligations. No single recipient or presentation format becomes a compulsory path for other consumers or for live trading.

## Neighbor Boundaries

SQMV describes source and derived meaning; SQTV performs requested conversion; SQFV moves bounded payloads and owns their physical lifetime; SQPV supplies proved retained ranges when selected. SQDV owns consumer selection, view binding, resume, and delivery-state interpretation at its admitted boundary. The receiving vector or destination integration retains authority for its own processing, result, remote commit, and queryability claims.

## Non-Scope

SQDV does not acquire source records, change source meaning, grant access by itself, retain data, simulate backtests, make model output canonical, publish private datasets, or place order or market-data handling on a live strategy's required path.
