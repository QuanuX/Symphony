# Symphony Quantitative Delivery Vector Skill

## Purpose

Keep each research-data recipient's selected view, authority, progress, and failure outcome explicit.

## Reading Order

1. `knowledge/ARCHITECTURE.md` and the SQV Contract Quad
2. this SQDV Quad and the exact SQMV meaning of the selected dataset and view
3. selected SQTV, SQFV, SQPV, receiving-vector, destination-integration, and access contracts

## Procedure

1. Identify the recipient, its actual receiving interface, dataset/view revision, required representation, scope, and supported delivery mode.
2. Resolve permission to collect, retain, transform, share, or export separately as the selected purpose requires.
3. Declare preview or committed status, completeness, known gaps, finite credits, retry and backfill policy, and exact resume context.
4. Use SQTV, SQFV, and SQPV for conversion, movement, and retained catch-up under their selected contracts.
5. Distinguish queue, transport, processing, acceptance, remote commit, queryability, and buffer-release evidence; test ambiguous acknowledgements and live-to-retained cutover.
6. Review exact adapter, schema, feature, installation, and namespace consequences before claiming recipient support.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before treating rights metadata as an access grant, a transport receipt as processing, preview as durable, a recipient's result as SQDV-owned truth, or a future external destination as already integrated.
