# Symphony Quantitative Vector Specification

## Framework Boundary

SQV components remove repeated engineering work only where Symphony has an explicitly ratified, reusable contract. A user may use, replace, extend, bypass, or combine available components according to the compatibility and authority boundaries of their installation.

A Nest serves a user-selected purpose and may contain many modular sections. The fact that single-purpose hot-path Nests are commonly elegant does not authorize SQV to prohibit another architecture.

## Research-Data Boundary

The research-data programme aims to acquire selected observations, preserve their meaning, prepare compatible representations, move bounded payloads, retain selected evidence, and deliver selected views. `knowledge/sqv/RESEARCH-DATA.md` aligns its six purposes and cross-owner obligations. SQAV owns source collection; SQMV owns attributable descriptions; SQFV owns bounded movement and buffer lifetime; SQTV owns selected conversions and derived transformations; SQPV owns selected retention and retrieval; SQDV owns selected consumer views and handoff. These are architecture owners, not six mandatory services or a claim that a runtime exists. SQV does not itself implement a universal dispatcher, provider parser, database, or transport.

First-party research-data data-plane implementations are native C++. An exact selected composition may use direct in-process transfer, a separate-process adapter, a network path, or retained retrieval as its admitted components permit. No central bus, canonical representation, compulsory conversion, or finite knowledge-engine envelope is imposed on bulk data. Administrative qxctl surfaces follow the user's later instructions; their future role does not make qxctl a routine payload relay.

## Thermal Boundary

SQV contracts must distinguish hot, warm, cool, cold, and freezing consequences where they matter. Administrative SKV, qxctl, graph, publication, agentic, research, and change-recording mechanisms must not silently become inline dependencies of hot or warm strategy execution. A real-time research stream does not thereby become part of a live execution Nest.

## Broker and Data Interfaces

Not every execution Nest needs a broker API or data API. Broker interfaces are primarily relevant to non-FIX systems and must preserve exact version compatibility when present. A Nest built against version 3 remains version-3-bound until the user explicitly migrates it; a current version 13 does not reinterpret the old contract. Independent research sources such as economic data and news do not inherit broker semantics.

FIX systems approach data, symbol matching, and messaging through their own regulated protocol family and SOOV architecture. Non-FIX broker API governance and version lineage remain distinct deferred work. A draft SCABV proposal is not proof that a broker adapter or operational credential path exists.

## Reusable Engines

Historical user-authored engines may later be decomposed by function into reusable example components and reassembled as coherent public engines. The Architect determines their names and approves function-to-vector ownership.

## Non-Authorization Statement

SQV defines no universal feed declaration, symbol universe, strategy schema, broker choice, data path, persistence adapter, exhaust record, synchronous/asynchronous rule, or order behavior. The admitted research-data owner Quads do not allocate a colon namespace, public module identity, release, provider access, or qxctl command.
