# Symphony Quantitative Metadata Vector Skill

## Purpose

Keep research-data descriptions exact, attributable, and separate from source observations and payload movement.

## Reading Order

1. `knowledge/ARCHITECTURE.md` and `knowledge/SLANG.md`
2. the SQV Contract Quad and this SQMV Quad
3. the exact source, transformation, flow, persistence, delivery, identity, and access contracts implicated by the selected dataset

## Procedure

1. Identify the producer of each metadata fact and its source, scope, revision, and evidence.
2. Distinguish dataset, schema, representation, time roles, coverage, lineage, rights, and consumer-view identities.
3. Record unknown or unsupported semantics explicitly; do not fill gaps from convenient defaults.
4. Resolve exact schema and representation versions before a consumer interprets a compact descriptor reference.
5. Keep operational metadata immutable for an admitted batch and avoid an inline catalogue dependency unless a later specific contract selects one.
6. Review namespace, SKVI, feature, and installation consequences only when a concrete family or implementation is proposed.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before inventing a provider observation, rights grant, complete dataset claim, identity grammar, universal schema, metadata service, or public module capability. Refer movement and lifetime to SQFV, and FIX framework semantics to SOOV.
