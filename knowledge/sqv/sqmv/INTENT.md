# Symphony Quantitative Metadata Vector Intent

## Purpose

The Symphony Quantitative Metadata Vector (SQMV) is SQV's owner of descriptive research-data contracts. It gives user-selected datasets, schemas, representations, time roles, provenance, coverage, lineage, and rights or access classifications an exact meaning that producers and consumers can share.

## Evidence Boundary

SQMV describes attributable facts supplied by their actual source or producer. It does not invent a source observation, infer complete coverage from a successful transfer, or grant a right that the source did not establish. A private dataset description does not become official Symphony knowledge merely by being indexed.

## Composition Boundary

SQMV makes descriptions available for selected research compositions. Operationally needed metadata may be resolved into an immutable descriptor or referenced manifest before a batch moves; a data record need not synchronously consult a metadata service. Users remain free to use, replace, or bypass Symphony-provided components under the contracts of their chosen composition.

## Non-Scope

SQMV does not acquire data, own bulk payload bytes or flow control, perform transformations, commit storage, deliver a recipient view, select a provider, or prescribe a live strategy's data path. SOOV retains FIX framework ownership.
