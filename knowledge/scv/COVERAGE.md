# SCV Maintained Provider Coverage Contract v1

## Scope and Ownership

The additive `0.5.0-dev` SCV, SCHV, SCEV and five provider engines expose `provider_coverage`, a pure query with result `symphony.scv.provider-coverage.v1`. qxctl administers it through `scv provider coverage`. The exact descriptor now has 22 operations. Earlier exact installations and success payloads retain their own interfaces.

SCV's C++ owner accounts for a declared source inventory, explicit corpus evidence selection and supplied authored interpretations. These dimensions remain separate. Declaring a source does not acquire it; acquiring bytes does not interpret every concept; a matched extraction does not establish semantic truth, present availability or a working deployment. The report does not score vendors, prescribe a provider or define a universal percentage of provider knowledge.

The selected provider inventory is a candidate produced by `provider_onboard`. It describes the caller's bounded reference scope, not a selected source head, exhaustive vendor census or publisher-certified catalog. Human and permitted agent authors use the same interface. The caller remains free to introduce private sources, alternate mappings, independent modules and unanticipated compositions.

## Exact Input and Replay

The payload is exactly `{provider,corpus_query,interpretations}`. `provider` is the sealed Provider v1 candidate. `corpus_query` is the complete existing native request `{corpus,query_time,member_ids,selection,max_age_seconds}`, containing the exact Corpus v1 object. It is distinct from the qxctl retained-store query request that selects a snapshot digest. Empty interpretation input explicitly declares that no authored interpretation evidence was supplied.

The owner regenerates the candidate through `provider_onboard`, recomputes the corpus query and replays every supplied provider interpretation from its retained captures, profiles, bindings and policy. Caller-supplied totals or a self-sealed wrapper alone are insufficient. Exact duplicate interpretation wrappers fail. Distinct wrappers may use different policies and local profile/claim identities; accounting does not compose them into a common-policy graph or merge repeated statements into independent corroboration.

A parent coverage engine may consume a subordinate corpus within its declared family/provider containment. The corpus query runs under the corpus's recorded domain, which remains visible. A child cannot consume a parent or unrelated family's corpus by relabeling it. This is pure validation of supplied domain artifacts, not evidence that a particular external provider executable was invoked. qxctl artifact provenance separately identifies the installed owner actually used to validate a retained record.

The explicit corpus query preserves latest-attempt disposition, selected latest-attempt or last-complete evidence, query time, freshness and source-revision agreement. Empty member IDs retain the existing meaning of all members in the supplied corpus. An empty corpus is valid evidence of an empty selected collection; it does not remove declared sources from the report.

## Inventory, Selection and Interpretation Axes

The report retains its exact input and complete native corpus query result. Each declared source/locator gets a row with its corpus membership, selection status, selected capture identity, declaration comparison and credited interpretation bindings. Declared locators outside the selected evidence remain visible. Selected members outside the declared provider/source/locator inventory are listed separately with the exact gap reason, and receive no declared-inventory credit.

Metadata indexes establish only their declared identity and structure. The coverage owner does not claim that a metadata-only capture is present in a retained store. A supplied interpretation can establish exact body availability for this request by replaying its captures; the owner regenerates the capture index and checks correspondence with the selected metadata before crediting that binding. A separate qxctl retained corpus inspection/query performs its existing deep-store verification. These distinct forms of evidence must not be conflated.

The provider inventory contains desired source fields, without a source generation or selected-head digest. Consequently declaration equality is reported only where a replayed capture supplies its full source declaration. Differences in publisher, role, scope, locators or continuity evidence remain inspectable; generation/sealing fields are excluded from this comparison. Matching a source/locator token in an index does not establish declaration equality or authorization of a relocation.

Each credited profile binding identifies the interpretation, profile and exact selected capture, plus its matched and unresolved rule IDs. Counts describe rule attempts under those specific authored profiles, not unique facts or supported runtime guarantees. A replayed historical or unlisted binding remains visible in `unselected_bindings` with its reason. It cannot become evidence about the newly selected capture merely because source identity is unchanged.

The summary independently counts declared sources and locators, selected and unselected inventory, selected unlisted members, replayed selected captures, profile bindings, matched/unresolved rule attempts and selected evidence status/freshness. It does not hide a failed latest refresh when the caller selects older complete evidence: the complete native query remains part of the result.

## qxctl and Retention

`scv provider coverage` selects an exact receipt-owned package, accepts the native input through `--input`, and supports `--json`. The consumer independently checks input identity, query binding, complete row and binding inventories, ordering, source/capture correspondence, partitions and recomputed counts. A resealed fabricated result must fail. The safe native rejection code is `coverage.invalid`; existing source/corpus/interpretation rejection codes remain applicable to their validation boundaries.

Exact packaged schema discovery includes the new input/result definitions, limits and an authoring template. The new template preserves the distinction between a candidate inventory, a native corpus query input and actual interpretation evidence. No source checkout is required to discover the installed protocol.

The existing artifact interface additionally retains `provider_onboard` results as kind `provider` and `provider_coverage` results as kind `coverage`. Import with a null result computes the owner result; a supplied result requires exact replay equality. Show replays the exact recorded owner. List remains envelope-only inspection. These are additive artifact kinds available through the new qxctl interface, not a change to old immutable records or an implicit upgrade of older readers. Coverage remains optional beside direct owner operations and retained workflows.

Retained `provider` and `coverage` records require exact `0.5.0-dev` installation provenance at import and read. Earlier direct `provider_onboard` invocations retain their existing version contracts; this retention rule does not upgrade those installations or their artifacts.

## Bounds and Verification

Existing bounds remain independent: a provider has one to 32 sources, each with one to 16 locators; a corpus has at most 128 members; coverage accepts at most 16 distinct interpretation wrappers. Each wrapper retains its existing capture/profile/rule bounds. At most 256 bindings and 2,048 rule attempts fit the count limits, subject to the unchanged process byte, value, depth, string and deadline limits. No overflow silently drops rows or evidence.

Producer and consumer tests must cover declared-but-unselected sources, selected unlisted evidence, partial/failed refresh and retained complete evidence, expired/future observations, metadata-only versus replayed bodies, historical unselected mappings, altered indexes or wrappers, source-declaration drift, domain containment, duplicate wrappers and independent interpretation policies. Installed-process acceptance must exercise the actual C++ owner through qxctl and preserve earlier exact installations.

The maintained-source example may include newly acquired public documents, dated reconnaissance entries and labeled hypothetical fixtures. Each keeps its own provenance and acquisition status. A bounded coverage report cannot promote unobserved sources, unknown service conditions or unresolved user requirements into provider facts. Source/graph selection, credentials, deployment, publication and general alternative synthesis remain separate operations or future work.
