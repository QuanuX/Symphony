# Symphony Cloud Vector Intent

## Purpose

The Symphony Cloud Vector (SCV) owns private semantic knowledge about offsite hardware resources and the provider realities that shape clusters, training, strategy development, research, machine learning, and data work.

## Scope

SCV maps provider resources, offerings, regions, infrastructure domains, constraints, observed availability, and hybrid possibilities. Its initial provider universe includes AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, DigitalOcean, and user-owned hardware where comparison with offsite resources is required.

## Sovereignty

SCV represents the user's declared constraints and observed provider facts. It does not rank providers, prefer a hyperscaler, or impose a Symphony boundary on what the user may attempt.

## Privacy

An installed SCV knowledge graph belongs to the private user or TOPS installation. User-supplied accounts, balances, permissions, contractual constraints, or other personal facts may be used only through their explicitly enabled observation and contract surfaces. Private facts do not enter official Symphony source truth.

## Relationships

- SOV is the operational control plane through which Symphony may act upon SCV evidence.
- SNV records the identities of resulting physical Nodes, resources, clusters, and names.
- SHV owns detailed hardware capability knowledge.
- SKV governs SCV contracts, indexing, change, evolution, and official-publication relationships.
