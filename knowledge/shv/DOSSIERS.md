# SHV explicit component dossiers v1

qxctl `shv dossier run|graph|schema|template` organizes caller associations to exact source-scoped subjects. Existing C++ source/kernel owners replay facts, coverage and requirements; the native generic graph adapter validates structural exchange. This increment adds orchestration only, with no new native interpretation or identity-resolution owner. No physical components or document subjects are merged.

Input exactly `{component,inventory,associations}`. Component exactly `{id,label}` is a caller identifier and display label, not a verified SKU. Inventory uses INVENTORY.md unchanged, including its exact profile, explicit endpoints, bounds and full evidence replay. Associations0..32 contain unique `{id,roster_id,relation,citation_digests}` entries. Each roster_id must exist. Relation is an arbitrary caller token, preserving extensibility; even a relation named same_component remains a caller claim. Citation digests0..8 per association are unique tagged SHA256 values. They hash canonical `{assertion:<complete native assertion>}` including source ID, predicate, qualifier and value. A citation is scoped by the selected roster subject and exact catalogue, not globally by a name or repeated value.

Run requires explicit --prefix/--version coverage owner and --input. It first replays the entire inventory through original native owners. It then rereads bounded bundles, verifies their whole self-seals and exact digest equality to the already replayed observations, and retains source subjects and native evaluations. A changed bundle rejects; rereading never substitutes a newer proof. Sources are sequential observations, not a global atomic snapshot.

Each association preserves its original subject and catalogue digest, citations and status. Missing subjects or mismatched roster labels yield subject_unresolved. Matched subjects with no selected citations yield caller_association. Selected citations all present yield source_cited_association; any missing yields citation_unresolved. Citation findings are exact_assertion_present or missing_assertion. An empty assertion list remains empty: catalogue membership can originate from caller mapping declarations and does not certify that a heading or hardware fact was extracted. Original assertions and divergent labels remain visible without field merging or conflict adjudication.

Every association says association_authority=caller and identity_verified=false. A source-cited association establishes that the selected assertion is present in the replayed catalogue; it does not establish that the citation logically entails the caller relation, that a vendor declared an alias, or that two records identify the same physical component. identity_resolution=not_performed is explicit. No undocumented inference, normalizing taxonomy, vendor authentication, transitive equivalence or selected winning source is introduced.

Output symphony.qxctl.shv-dossier.v1 retains the complete request, inventory result, association records and each evidence bundle’s replayed native evaluation. Requirements belong to their original subject and bundle; they are not applied across the caller group or silently combined. Default coverage and unknown introduction dates remain native and caller-configurable. The result is sealed and catalogue_published=false. Schema and unanswered template discovery require no installations; --json covers success and errors.

Graph additionally requires --adapter-prefix/--adapter-version. It builds a generic exchange projection owned by qxctl-shv-dossier version1 (an orchestration artifact owner, not a new installed engine). The complete dossier remains owner_artifact. One CallerComponent node and one CallerAssociation node per association are joined only by caller_associates edges; the caller relation is a property, never a verified equivalence edge. Nodes/edges sort by ID; the graph seal and unchanged native adapter roundtrip are checked independently by qxctl. The output retains dossier, transport and exact adapter installation. Graph storage and publication are separate operations.

Vendor-evidenced alias entailment requires a future exact native contract and supported source interpretation. This dossier is a precise way to retain candidate associations and relevant facts without claiming that future capability exists.
