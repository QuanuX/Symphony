# SHV source lifecycle engine intent

Provide independently installable C++ source-revision planning, immutable capture provenance, explicit change comparison and source-replayed graph exchange. Sources describe original components, families or evidenced variants; users retain coverage and tool choice through qxctl. Plans and pure transitions do not activate a registry or approve a location. Reuse authority-free Phase1 process/path/digest/receipt mechanics with SHV source ownership.
