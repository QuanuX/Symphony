# SQAV Capture C++ Intent

Preserve original source bytes together with bounded, attributable acquisition
evidence for explicitly selected offline/native adapters. Keep exact interface
and adapter versions, acquisition attempts, native positions, timestamp roles,
coverage assertions and optional source record counts distinct.

Offer an optional C++26 capture representation that composes with SQMV metadata,
SQFV movement and selected retention/delivery paths. Source acquisition policy,
provider parsing, credentials and source access remain their selected owners'
contracts. Users may choose other representations and integrations.
