# Symphony Backtesting Vector engine

Provide independently installable C++26 historical experiment execution and complete portable evidence under user-selected criteria. A causal signal census precedes execution observations, studies and replay extraction. qxctl controls every implemented operation and presents all retained data without a GUI dependency.

This first experimental release implements a bounded vertical slice of the October 5 SBV design. It does not claim the complete white-paper roadmap, universal strategy support, calibrated execution inference, competitor parity or performance leadership.
