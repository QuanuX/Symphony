# SEV Engine Intent

## Status

Implemented development foundation.

## Purpose

`symphony-sev` is the independently installable C++26 freezing-path implementation of SEV and its first SCSEV profile. It opens evidence-bound cases, assesses caller-declared impacts, derives dependency-safe ready sets, verifies reobserved outcomes, recalculates successor cases, provides non-mutating recovery/closure proposals, and evaluates all fourteen qxctl consequence families.

## Boundaries

The engine never applies a plan, persists a session, invents identities or grammar, grants authority, bypasses hard safety edges, creates a second command registry, listens on a network, or participates in hot/warm work.
