# SCIV engine intent

The independently installable SCIV C++26 engine evaluates supplied cluster, membership and bus evidence. It distinguishes a desired grouping, participation facts, observed connectivity and historical relationships. The caller identifies the system, cluster, Nodes and applicable incarnations; SCIV supplies no identifiers or authority to operate them.

Its semantic library supports direct static consumers and the thin SNV parent's exact replay. `sciv_validate` produces a reproducible evidence finding; `sciv_transition` produces a digest-bound proposed addition for subsequent retention. Both are finite, nonresident operations.
